    How this list is made

    A scraper runs at 6 AM and 3 PM IST against each company's own careers site (Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Oracle and similar). Titles match on data-engineering roles (Data Engineer, Analytics Engineer, Big Data, Software Engineer – Data and the reverse word order) or a skill word in the title (Spark, PySpark, Hadoop, ETL, Databricks, Airflow, dbt, Snowflake, data warehouse or pipeline). Abbreviations such as Sr., Eng. and Dev are expanded first. Locations include neighbourhoods (Hinjewadi, Kharadi, Powai, Goregaon, Airoli and others) and remote roles open in India. Internships, data-centre, data-entry and data-scientist roles are left out.

    Job titles link to the posting itself. The list is rebuilt twice a day, so links are fresh; a role that closes drops off at the next scan. For companies without a usable careers feed, or whose site failed that day, roles come from LinkedIn's public job search, checked about twice a day, and are marked via LinkedIn. Every other company is also searched on LinkedIn every day or two; a role LinkedIn shows that the careers-site scan didn't give is added and marked via LinkedIn · missed by careers site. Roles with no date on the careers site take the date from the posting page or LinkedIn where possible. Rows marked check passed the filters on a judgement call, such as a remote role that doesn't name a country.
